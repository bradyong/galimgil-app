# Writer experiment: quality target not met

## Boundaries

Feature branch only. No deploy, main merge, push, Android or ad changes.
The user approved a single-response semantic/Writer experiment, at most 20 paid
evaluations and USD 0.05. Exactly 20 requests ran, without retry. Actual usage:
24,397 input tokens, 4,246 output tokens, zero cached tokens. At published
gpt-5-mini rates, USD 0.01459125. Billing statement not inspected.

## Architecture

- `choice_writer.py` defines the separate writing schema, prompt and validation.
  It cannot call a provider, score or classify anything.
- New requests in `choice_meaning.py` request `{meaning, writer}` in ONE response.
  Existing semantic-v2 validation, normalization and scoring remain unchanged.
- The offline paid experiment fixes the saved semantic-v2 structures and requests
  only Writer output. It does NOT prove that fresh integrated semantic generation
  has unchanged quality/latency/cost. Integrated single-call behavior is mock-tested.
- In `app.js`, `writeSemanticContent` receives the final deterministic decision.
  Writer data never enters `buildChoiceMeaning`, its hash, features or scores.
  Both potential endings are cached separately in contentSources.writerPayload.
- Archive reuse does not call AI again to upgrade old writing. Old archives stay
  intact and may still use legacy drafts.
- Model-written reasons showed invented benefits and social outcomes. They remain
  in the raw report but are NOT rendered. Local reasons describe verified activities
  and state the actual card/zodiac or playful tie-break basis.
- Invalid writing does not invalidate meaning, block play or request another call.
  It falls back to existing scene drafts and is marked `legacy-draft` internally.

## Results: same 28 cases, no replacements

- Meaning and decisionEvidence identical on all 28; 30 seeds each (840 comparisons).
  Winners, both scores, stars and final text unchanged.
- All eight rule-handled outputs remain byte-for-byte identical, including food fun.
- Inline confirmation remains 2/28 (tea and invented terms).
- Writer packages: 5 accepted, 13 rejected, 2 blocked by meaning confirmation.
  Acceptance is structural, not an assurance of humor or factual entailment.
- Rendered outputs (26): internal-code exposure 2 -> 0.
- Predictive future markers: 16 -> 11. Label-like caption heuristic: 17 -> 13.
  These are insufficient improvements and should NOT be described as success.
- Masked reason-template duplicate excess worsened 7/26 -> 22/26 because of the
  conservative local reason scaffold. Future remains 0/26; caption remains 1/26.
  Identical full-sentence duplicate excess stays zero in each role.

## Failure evidence

Raw responses frequently use nominal captions despite explicit instructions.
Some anchors quote the old draft rather than the validated summary/activity.
Some reasons invent cost benefit, faster flow, improved concentration, or another
person's response. Even accepted packages retain formal or bland narration and
some speculative details; full human quality review is still needed.
Do not weaken validation merely to raise the acceptance rate, add per-case jokes,
or patch a word dictionary. No additional paid retries were used to hide failures.
